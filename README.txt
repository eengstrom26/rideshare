Description:
This program uses the files of Passenger, Car, Station, Road and RideshareTester to determine the goal: "If there are 50 randomly generated passengers, what percentage of them make it to their destination if there 20 cars? What percentage of passengers make it if there are 40 cars?"

Rideshare simulates a road with 32 stations where cars move in one direction to their destination. Once a car reaches their destination, the car is terminated, and the passengers get off at that station. A car may pick up passengers waiting at a station depending on if they have space and if they are going in the same direction. A car can have a max of 3 passengers in its car. Both passengers and cars start at a random station. The simulation tracks how many passengers make it to their desitnations. 

Results:
20 Car, 50 Passenger scenario: about 40% - 45% of passengers make it
40 Car, 50 Passenger scenario: about 50% - 60% of passengers make it

These results can be run in RideshareTester, where each time it runs the program gives the average of 100 trials. In addition, I have included a step by step portion that shows the car and station at each step of one random scenario. Either can be viewed if the other is commented out.
